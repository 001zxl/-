# Ecommerce Image Design System

## 1. Product Truth Layer

Lock the factual product identity before design:

- Brand and logo.
- Product category and SKU.
- Packaging type, shape, material, closure, and transparent/opaque areas.
- Main visible color palette and product texture.
- Verified claims from package/user copy only.

If the product photo has weak lighting or clutter, still use it as identity reference. Improve the scene, not the product identity.

## 2. Platform Layer

Choose the visual behavior by platform:

- **Douyin/TikTok ecommerce**: large hook text, fast readability, high contrast, real product scale, sticker/callout language, livestream energy.
- **Tmall/Taobao/JD**: trust, clarity, benefit hierarchy, clean modules, proof and specification.
- **Xiaohongshu**: lifestyle, real use scene, soft editorial rhythm, less hard selling.
- **Amazon/Shopify**: clean product proof, comparison, feature clarity, low clutter, minimal risky text.

## 3. Category Adaptation Layer

Choose proof and visual language by category before choosing style:

- **Food/snack/beverage**: texture, appetite, freshness, serving scene, transparent packaging, portion sense. Avoid unverified health claims.
- **Beauty/skincare/personal care**: ingredient mood, skin-feel texture, routine scene, before/after only if provided and compliant, clean premium packaging. Avoid medical or guaranteed-effect claims.
- **Apparel/accessories**: fit, fabric texture, model/body context, size guide, styling combinations, close-up stitching. Preserve color accuracy.
- **Electronics/digital devices**: feature callouts, compatibility, ports, screen UI, scale, scenario desk setup, comparison tables. Avoid fake certifications or impossible specs.
- **Home/lifestyle goods**: room context, scale, material, storage/use method, before/after organization, family or daily routine cues.
- **Baby/pet**: safety, softness, material, real use scene, trust symbols only if verified. Avoid unsupported safety/certification badges.
- **Health/supplement**: packaging clarity, ingredient display, routine context, compliance-heavy copy. Avoid disease, cure, guaranteed outcome, and exaggerated body-effect claims.
- **Tools/outdoor/auto**: durability, use scenario, size, material, step-by-step installation, comparison, rugged proof.
- **Digital/service/local service**: interface mockups, result preview, process steps, social proof, conversion CTA. Avoid pretending screenshots or ratings are real unless provided.

If the category is uncertain, ask one concise question or infer conservatively from the product image and avoid risky claims.

## 4. Image Type Layer

Use one job per image:

- **Main image**: product recognition and click hook.
- **Detail first screen**: answer what it is and why buy.
- **Feature proof**: one claim with visual evidence.
- **Packaging/trust**: show real material, closure, transparency, label, size.
- **Macro/detail**: texture, ingredient, craftsmanship, material.
- **Variant/group**: flavors, bundles, choice.
- **Scene**: when/where/who uses it.
- **Promo/cover**: short-video or livestream attention.

## 5. Layout System

For every image define:

- Subject scale: product should usually occupy 40%-70% of frame unless the image is macro or scene-led.
- Text hierarchy: one headline, one subhead, up to three labels.
- Reading path: top-to-bottom or left-to-right; avoid scattered text islands.
- Mobile readability: headline must still read at thumbnail size.
- Product safety zone: do not cover logo, key package text, closure, or product window.

## 6. Style Territories

Rotate territories to avoid fatigue:

### A. Clean Trust

White/gray/soft gradient, product centered, sparse gold/black lines, specification callouts.

Use for: trust, platform-neutral detail pages, high-product-accuracy modules.

### B. Douyin Viral Sticker

Bright blocks, thick black titles, red/yellow/cyan accents, sticker labels, motion cues.

Use for: livestream covers, young audience, click-oriented modules.

### C. Macro Proof

Close crop, shallow depth, magnifier, texture callout, minimal props.

Use for: food texture, material quality, transparent packaging, ingredient proof.

### D. Premium Dark

Charcoal/black background, rim light, gold accents, glossy shadows, restrained type.

Use for: upgraded brand feeling, premium SKU, hero detail module.

### E. Editorial Lifestyle

Natural scene, restrained props, warm/cool mood by audience, product remains dominant.

Use for: use-case page, Xiaohongshu-ish softer ecommerce, family/office/party scenes.

### F. Process/Origin

Flow cards, raw material, tool/process props, numbered steps.

Use for: craftsmanship, trust, manufacturing story, traditional process.

### G. Variant Shelf

SKU lineup, color blocking, clean labels, product family hierarchy.

Use for: flavors, bundles, cross-sell, store carousel.

## 7. Typography

- Hook headline: thick sans, display type, or brand-appropriate calligraphy. Avoid using the same title style across the entire batch.
- Detail labels: medium sans, high contrast, short phrases.
- Legal/spec text: do not ask image generation to recreate tiny package copy. Use original product photos or post-production compositing.

## 8. Color System

Build a palette per territory:

- Base: background and scene.
- Product anchor: derived from real packaging.
- Accent: one high-attention color.
- Neutral: white/black/gray for readability.

Avoid using one palette for a long sequence unless the brand needs strict unity. For a 6-10 image batch, rotate at least three background styles.

## 9. Claims And Compliance

Avoid unsupported:

- Health/medical effects.
- Absolute superiority, rankings, "best", "number one", "全网最低", "销量第一".
- Origin, organic, additive-free, hand-made, or special process unless verified.
- Fake discounts or platform badges unless provided.

Use safer phrasing:

- "看得见", "颗粒饱满", "开袋即食", "方便储存", "适合分享", "原香酥脆" when supported by product/category context.

## 10. Agent Blind-Spot Checklist

Proactively check for details the user may not mention:

- Aspect ratio and platform crop requirements.
- Whether generated text should be final or placeholder for later compositing.
- Whether product photos must be preserved exactly.
- Required SKU/spec/flavor/version distinctions.
- Category-specific compliance risks.
- Brand consistency across a batch.
- Visual fatigue from repeated palette, prop, font, or layout.
- Whether a "quiet trust" image is needed after loud promotional images.
- Whether a "proof" image is needed after lifestyle images.
- Whether the image should sell click-through, trust, education, comparison, or checkout conversion.

## 11. QA Checklist

Before final delivery:

1. Product resembles the real reference.
2. Brand is not changed.
3. Main selling point is obvious in 2 seconds.
4. Each image has a distinct role and style.
5. Text is readable and not overcrowded.
6. Props support the buying reason.
7. Background does not hide product edges.
8. No risky claims or fake data.
